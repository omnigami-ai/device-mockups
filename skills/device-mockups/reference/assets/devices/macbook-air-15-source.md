Changes (Blender 5.2, `prepare-air.py` beside this file): node transforms baked; the Apple mark on the lid
deleted; the screen's macOS wallpaper image dropped (the screen gets a plain material, replaced by the
product's capture in the film); parts named for the device contract (`SCREEN`, `GLASS`, `BEZEL`, `LID_BACK`,
`LID_GASKET`, `CAMERA_*`, `BASE_*`); the whole laptop pitched about its hinge by the lid's 20.07° lean so the
screen faces glTF +Z exactly. Hinge line at glTF (y −0.0073, z −0.1142); the lid opens 110.07° from the base.
Apple product names describe the device shape only; no Apple mark, wallpaper or screen ships.
